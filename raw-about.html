About MaqAmir

A studio in your pocket — multitrack recording, mixing, a synth lab, AI production tools, and cloud sharing, all running natively in the browser with sample-accurate timing, minimum latency, and studio-grade fidelity. No plugins to install.

MaqAmir is built on the Web Audio API. The whole engine — every piano voice, every synth oscillator, every drum machine sample, every FX node — is scheduled against audioContext.currentTime so that nothing drifts, nothing skips, and nothing depends on the JavaScript event loop for timing.

It began because the standard 12-tone equal-temperament keyboard cannot play the music a large part of the world actually listens to. The microtonal piano, the maqam scales, and the per-octave tuning controls were the project's first reason to exist — and they remain a freedom no other DAW offers, always available and never mandatory. Around that core grew a complete production environment: the recording studio, the multitrack mixer, the synth lab, the drum kits, and the AI tools that together make MaqAmir a full DAW you can carry anywhere.

Cultural & Musical Heritage

MaqAmir's microtonal piano and cultural scale library draw on centuries of work by musicians, theorists, and communities far older than any software project. We acknowledge in particular:

  • The Maqam tradition
    Arabic, Turkish, and Persian modal systems that gave the project its name.
  • Carnatic & Hindustani classical traditions
    Raga structures and microtonal practice from the Indian subcontinent.
  • Just Intonation & Equal Temperament researchers
    Tuning systems and historical temperaments referenced in the Scale Library.
  • Solfeggio frequency tradition
    Healing-frequency presets used in the Heal mode.
