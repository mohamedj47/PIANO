1.1Music Creation3 min

Whisper-Soft Fingertips to Bold Chords & Instant MIDI Drop

What You'll Create

Play an expressive melody in under a minute with natural touch sensitivity — or drop any MIDI song straight onto the keys.

  1. 1Open Piano Studio with Maqam Rast active and play any row of white keys — every note is already tuned to sound harmonious together.
  2. 2Tap lightly with your fingertip for a delicate, intimate tone, then press with the flat pad of your finger to feel rich concert-grand fullness.
  3. 3Have a favorite .mid song file on your computer? Drag and drop it anywhere onto the Piano Studio screen to hear it perform live with sustain and pitch bends.
Try This Creative Twist:Tap the Sparkles (Magic) button in the top bar to deal a fresh combination of instrument, scale, and atmosphere in one tap.

1.2Experimentation3 min

Singing Chords: Bending One Melody Note Inside a Held Chord

What You'll Create

Make a single note cry, shimmer, or bend like a human voice while the rest of your chord stays rock-solid.

  1. 1Hold down a two- or three-note chord on screen, keeping your left-hand notes steady.
  2. 2Without lifting your melody finger, slide slowly left or right to bend pitch smoothly, or wiggle gently for warm acoustic vibrato.
  3. 3Slide that same finger upward on the key to open brightness and shimmer, or glide across the lower edge of the keys for a flowing harp glissando.
Try This Creative Twist:Switch to Oudora (Desert Oud) or Cellomir and slide between adjacent notes for vocal-like Middle Eastern phrasing.

1.3Sound Design3 min

Hands-Free Drones & Two-Finger Stereo Placement Lock

What You'll Create

Lock a warm bass drone into the left side of your headphones so both hands are completely free to improvise on top.

  1. 1Touch a low C3 or G3 key with two stationary fingers for a brief moment (or right-click with a mouse) until the key locks on hands-free.
  2. 2On touchscreens, drag two fingers slightly left on that held key to place the drone warmly toward your left speaker, or upward to add rich saturation, then lift both fingers to lock that snapshot.
  3. 3Improvise freely with both hands in the upper octaves; tap or right-click the latched key once whenever you want it to fade out naturally.
Try This Creative Twist:On a computer mouse, hold Left-Click on a chord and tap Right-Click to latch multiple notes effortlessly.

1.4Innovation3 min

Physical Device Tilt, Dual Keyboards & Custom Key Sizing

What You'll Create

Turn your phone or tablet tilt into an expressive filter pedal and tailor two stacked keyboards to your exact hand span.

  1. 1Open Command Center (Settings gear icon) and enable Tilt Motion to sweep warmth or gentle vibrato simply by tilting your phone or tablet as you play.
  2. 2Open Piano Realm or tap Two Keyboards in Settings to stack an upper melody manual above a lower bass manual.
  3. 3Pinch two fingers on the octave bar (or adjust Key Zoom from 50% to 250%) so every key matches your fingers comfortably.
Try This Creative Twist:Put warm Upright Bass on the bottom keyboard and Arabic Qanun on the top keyboard for full two-handed concert play.
