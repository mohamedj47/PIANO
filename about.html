A Studio in Your Pocket

About MaqAmir

MaqAmir is a pocket studio built for musicians, composers, and producers who refuse to be boxed into 12-tone equal temperament. Combining cloud and AI production with minimum latency, studio-grade fidelity, and limitless microtonality, MaqAmir brings centuries of global musical heritage into a modern, zero-compromise instrument.

01 / Performance

Minimum Latency

Engineered around low-latency Web Audio synthesis and hardware-accelerated touch response so every ornament, quarter-tone bend, and percussive strike feels immediate.

02 / Audio Engine

Studio-Grade Fidelity

High-resolution multi-sampled instruments, real-time convolution reverb, and precision dynamics processing deliver mix-ready sound directly from your browser or mobile device.

03 / Tuning

Limitless Microtonality

Explore Arabic Maqam, Turkish Makam, Persian Dastgah, Indian Raga, and custom cent-level tunings with instant scale switching and visual pitch feedback.

04 / Workflow

Cloud & AI Production

Sync projects across devices, collaborate seamlessly, and harness AI-assisted arrangement and stem tools built specifically for microtonal and modal music.
